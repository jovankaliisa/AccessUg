### About The Developer - My Story

My name is Jovan Kaliisa, I am 16 years old and I am an S3 student in rural Uganda, currently in Masaka, Nkoni. I am writing this from a small village school area where many schools still use paper books to manage students, where records get lost when it rains, and where teachers walk long distances to submit reports. I do not have a laptop and I come from a humble background, but I have a dream to change how rural schools in Uganda are managed using technology. For the last three months, after my S3 classes, I have been coding on my small Android phone using Spck Editor to build a complete School Management System called ACCESS UG SCH. I built it because I saw the pain of my own teachers in Masaka Nkoni struggling with attendance books and I thought, why can't a rural school manage like a city school, digitally, on a phone? So I built everything on my phone. I built a Welcome Page that says Access. Choose. Succeed. to show that education is about access, I built a secure Portal Login where students, teachers and admins log in with Email, Password and Role, I built an Admin Dashboard that shows Total Schools, Total Students and Teachers, I built a Teacher Dashboard for Katumba Francis from ARCHBISHOP KIWANUKA SECONDARY Sch-001 S3 North where he can mark attendance and track behaviour A15 B12 C9 D6 E0, I built Student Management and Reports that show teacher absence and student performance instantly, and I made it ready for Supabase online database. All this is 13 full screens you can see in my Screenshots folder, all coded on a phone with no laptop, only data and determination. I am now humbly requesting for universal support, from MTN Foundation, from any organization, from any kind person who believes in rural Ugandan youth. I need a laptop, even a used one, so I can deploy this system to five schools here in Masaka Nkoni for free, train teachers, add Mobile Money for fees and SMS for parents, and keep building solutions for rural Uganda. Your support will not just help me Jovan Kaliisa the S3 student, it will help hundreds of rural students whose records will be safe and whose schools will manage better. Please test my live system, view my screenshots, and support this rural dream.
